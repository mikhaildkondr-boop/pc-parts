Expert PC Build Guides & Compatibility Tips

Essential knowledge for assembling, budgeting, and optimizing your custom PC.

CPU & Socket Matching

AMD AM5 supports Ryzen 7000/9000 with DDR5 only. Intel LGA1700 supports 12th–14th Gen. Check BIOS versions for newer chips.

Power Supply Headroom

Add 150W+ above estimated system draw for transient power spikes. Modern GPUs (RTX 40-series) benefit from ATX 3.0 PCIe 5.0 cables.

Secondary Market Safety

When buying used on OLX, Subito, or eBay, verify buyer protection, inspect GPU fan bearings, and run stress tests (FurMark/Cinebench).

Storage & RAM Sweet Spots

DDR5-6000 CL30 is optimal for Ryzen 7000/9000. For OS & games, PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSDs provide instant loading and DirectStorage support.
